Secured card = FD ke against card:
1️⃣ ₹50,000 ki FD pe limit aksar ₹40–45K (80–90%, bank pe depend)
2️⃣ Income proof aur CIBIL history aksar zaroori nahi, aur FD pe interest milta rehta hai

Par asli game hai secured se normal (unsecured) card tak pahunchna 👀 Uska 5-step plan hai, aur last step pe galti ki toh FD lien mein phansi reh sakti hai. Plan aur popular FD-backed cards website pe.
