CIBIL score hi nahi hai, toh card kaise milega? 🤷 FD wala card dekha hai? 👇

Secured vs unsecured credit card, seedha comparison:

🔒 Secured card (FD ke against)
• FD rakhni padti hai, bank us pe lien lagata hai
• Limit aksar FD ka 80–90% (kuch cards pe 100%)
• Income proof aur credit history aksar zaroori nahi
• FD pe interest milta rehta hai

💳 Unsecured card (normal card)
• Koi deposit nahi
• Limit tumhari income aur CIBIL pe depend
• Approval ke liye income proof + achha credit record chahiye

✅ Toh karna kya hai? Pehla card nahi mil raha? Secured card lo, bill hamesha poora aur time pe bharo. Achha record banne ke baad unsecured card ke liye apply karo.
