Student ho, salary nahi hai… phir bhi credit card mil sakta hai? Haan, 3 tareeke hain 🎓👇

Pehla credit card kaise milega:

1️⃣ FD ke against card: sabse common. FD rakho, uske against limit milti hai
2️⃣ Parent ke card pe add-on: unke account pe tumhara card. Bill ki zimmedari primary cardholder ki
3️⃣ Bank ki student scheme: kuch banks students ke liye alag card dete hain, rules bank pe depend

📋 Aksar chahiye: 18+ age, PAN, address proof, aur bank ke documents

✅ Card mil gaya? Toh:
• Bill hamesha poora aur time pe
• Limit ka chhota hissa hi use karo
• Ek saath 4–5 jagah apply mat karo
