Chai wale ke QR pe credit card se pay? Haan, ho sakta hai, agar card RuPay hai ☕💳👇

RuPay credit card ko UPI se link karna, simple steps:

1️⃣ Koi bhi UPI app kholo (BHIM, GPay, PhonePe, Paytm…)
2️⃣ "Add RuPay credit card" chuno, apna bank select karo
3️⃣ Card ke last 6 digits + expiry daalo, OTP verify karo
4️⃣ Naya UPI PIN set karo, savings account wale se alag rakho

⚠️ Dhyan rakho:
• Sirf merchant payments chalte hain (dukaan ka QR, online shopping). Dost ko P2P transfer, cash withdrawal, card-to-card nahi chalta
• Abhi UPI pe sirf RuPay credit cards link hote hain, Visa/Mastercard nahi
• Rewards UPI spends pe milenge ya nahi, bank aur card pe depend karta hai
• Aasaan payment = aasaan overspending. Bill poora bharna mat bhoolna
