"Sir, aapka card block ho jayega, bas OTP bata dijiye" 📞 Call kaat do. Seedha. 👇

Card fraud se bachne ka simple checklist:

1️⃣ OTP, CVV, PIN, UPI PIN kabhi kisi ko mat batao. Bank kabhi nahi maangta
2️⃣ Call pe koi screen-share ya "support" app install karwaye? Mat karo
3️⃣ "Reward points expire ho rahe hain" wala SMS link = aksar phishing
4️⃣ Bank app mein online/international usage off rakho jab zaroorat na ho, aur limit set karo (RBI ne yeh switch dena zaroori kiya hai)

🚨 Fraud ho gaya? Card turant block karo, bank ko turant report karo, aur 1930 pe call ya cybercrime.gov.in pe complaint karo.
✅ RBI rule: galti na tumhari ho na bank ki, aur bank ke alert ke 3 working days ke andar report kiya, toh liability zero.
