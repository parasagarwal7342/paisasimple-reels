Emergency mein ATM se credit card se cash nikaalne wale ho? 🏧 Ruko, pehle yeh dekho 👇

2 charges jo seedha lagte hain:
1️⃣ Cash withdrawal fee: aksar ~2.5%, minimum fee ke saath, upar se 18% GST
2️⃣ Interest usi din se. Koi interest-free period nahi

Aur interest kitna, kaise calculate hota hai? ₹15,000 wala example dekhoge toh shock lagega 👀 Card ka interest bilkul na dena pade, uske 5 tareeke website pe.
