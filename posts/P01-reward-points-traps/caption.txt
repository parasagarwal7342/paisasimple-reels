Sachte ho 1 reward point = ₹1? Kai cards pe milte hain sirf 20–30 paise 👇

Reward points aur cashback utne "free" nahi jitna ads dikhate hain:

1️⃣ 1 point ka value card pe depend karta hai. Statement credit pe kai baar 20–30 paise hi milte hain, travel pe better ho sakta hai
2️⃣ Fuel, EMI, wallet load, rent, government payments pe aksar 0 points
3️⃣ Kai cards pe points 2–3 saal mein expire ho jaate hain. Card cancel kiya, ya kuch banks pe 365 din use nahi kiya — points gaye
4️⃣ "5% cashback" aksar selected categories + monthly cap ke saath aata hai. Redemption pe bhi cap ho sakti hai

✅ Toh karna kya hai? Apne card ke T&Cs mein yeh 3 cheezein dekho: point ka paisa value, excluded spends, aur expiry. Extra kharch karke points mat kamao.
