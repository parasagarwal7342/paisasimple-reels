Lounge access ke myths vs facts:

❌ "Card hai toh lounge free" → ✅ Kai cards pe ab access pichhle mahine/quarter ke spend se unlock hota hai
❌ "Unlimited visits" → ✅ Aksar har quarter ya saal mein fixed visits
❌ "Dost bhi free jayega" → ✅ Guest ka aksar charge lagta hai, jab tak card mein guest visits na hon
❌ "Domestic hai toh international bhi" → ✅ International ke liye alag benefit chahiye hota hai

✅ Toh karna kya hai? Travel se pehle card ki website pe lounge list, spend condition aur bacha hua visit count check karo.
