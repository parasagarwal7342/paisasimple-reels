Same card, same bill… phir bhi kisi ko 50 din free milte hain, kisi ko sirf 20. Kaise? 👇

Sab billing cycle ka khel hai:

📅 Maan lo cycle 16 Oct se 15 Nov. Statement 15 Nov ko, due date 5 Dec (example)
1️⃣ 16 Oct ko kharcha kiya → 5 Dec tak bharna hai = ~50 din free
2️⃣ 15 Nov ko kharcha kiya → 5 Dec tak = sirf ~20 din
3️⃣ Interest-free days tabhi milte hain jab pichhla bill poora bhara ho
4️⃣ Cash withdrawal pe koi free days nahi, interest pehle din se

✅ Toh karna kya hai? Badi shopping statement date ke agle din karo, aur due date pe full amount ka auto-pay laga do.
