Card "free" hai, rewards bhi milte hain… toh bank kamata kaise hai? 🤔👇

Bank ki kamaai ke 4 bade raaste hain. Pehle 2:
1️⃣ Merchant fee (MDR): har swipe pe dukaandaar ek chhoti fee deta hai, uska hissa bank ko
2️⃣ Interest: bill poora nahi bhara toh kai cards pe ~3.5–3.75% har mahine

Teesra aur chautha raasta tumhari jeb se chupke se nikalta hai 👀 Aur asli sawaal: tumhare rewards ka paisa aakhir deta kaun hai? Jawab website pe hai, aur bank ko sabse kam paisa dene ke 6 tareeke bhi.
