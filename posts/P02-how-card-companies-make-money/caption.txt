Card "free" hai, rewards bhi milte hain… toh bank kamata kaise hai? 🤔👇

Sach bataun? Bank ki kamaai ke 4 bade raaste:

1️⃣ Merchant fee (MDR): har swipe pe dukaandaar ek chhoti fee deta hai, uska hissa card bank ko jaata hai
2️⃣ Interest: bill poora nahi bhara toh kai cards pe ~3.5–3.75% har mahine, yaani saal ka 40%+
3️⃣ Fees: annual fee, late fee, EMI processing fee
4️⃣ Forex markup aur cash withdrawal: videsh ke kharche pe aksar 1–3.5% extra, aur cash nikala toh fee + pehle din se interest

✅ Toh karna kya hai? Bill hamesha poora aur time pe bharo, cash mat nikaalo, aur fees ka hisaab rakho. Phir card sach mein "free" hai.
